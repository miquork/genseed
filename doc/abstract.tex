In high pileup a generated jet and the jet the detector reconstructs from the
same interaction are not the same object: PUPPI keeps part of the pileup and
removes part of the interaction's own energy, and any pairing rule between the
two collections --- a $\Delta R$ cut, a ghost assignment --- leaves a response
whose width and tails describe the pairing as much as the detector. genseed
links every generated particle of every interaction to the particle-flow
candidate(s) that carry its energy (tracks one to one on charge, direction,
momentum and $z$; calorimeter deposits at the parallax-corrected, bent impact
point) and uses the links in both directions. The \emph{GenJetSeed} of a
reconstructed jet is the generated energy of the candidates inside it, each
particle entering with its partner's PUPPI weight at generated scale, so that
the response $\ptraw/\ptlink$ is a pure detector response. The
\emph{RecoJetSeed} of a generated jet is the PUPPI jet clustered from the
candidates linked to its particles at the interaction's own vertex, so that
$\ptrs/\ptpure$ is what the detector made of that jet, with no pairing at all.
On a NeutrinoGun sample with $\langle N_{\mathrm{PU}}\rangle = 45$ the
GenJetSeed response at $|\eta|<0.5$, 5--6\,GeV has
$\sigma_{\mathrm{core}}/\mu = 0.27$ against 0.43 for a $\Delta R<0.2$ pairing
and $P(R>1.5) = 0.028$ against 0.095, with a Gaussian core that holds the
whole distribution ($f_{\mathrm{core}} = 1.00$; table~\ref{tab:gs_ladder}) and
a median that stays within 0.03 of the inclusive one in three
$N_{\mathrm{PU}}$ slices; a RecoJetSeed exists for 93\% of the pure generated
jets at 5--6\,GeV and $|\eta|<2.5$, against 60\% with a $\Delta R<0.2$
partner (table~\ref{tab:gs_efficiency}). In the lowptjets v4 study this package grew out of,
a two-step unfolding through the GenJetSeed closed at the per-cent level where
a single step sat 10--15\% high; that unfolding is not part of this package.
The package is self-contained: its own tiled
anti-kT clusterer, its own per-vertex PUPPI, a TTreeReader input layer, a
compact tuple of all four jet collections, a histogram file of the key
metrics, and this document, generated from them.
