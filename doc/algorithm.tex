\section{The problem}
\label{s:problem}

An inclusive jet measurement needs a response matrix from generated to
reconstructed jets, and the matrix needs a pairing. In a bunch crossing with
45 interactions, PUPPI run for one vertex hypothesis reconstructs the jets of
that interaction with part of the pileup left in and part of the interaction's
own soft neutrals floored out. A generated jet paired to the nearest
reconstructed jet inside $\Delta R < 0.2$ then has a response
$R = \ptraw/\ptpure$ whose left tail is truncated by the pairing (a badly
reconstructed jet moves out of the cone) and whose right tail is the pileup
the seed does not know about. Pairing by ghost association --- clustering the
generated particles as ghosts with the candidates and reading off which jet
took them --- makes the pairing complete but does not remove the right tail,
because the seed still does not know what PUPPI kept.

The observation behind genseed is that the pairing should be made at the
level where the ambiguity is smallest: between generated particles and
particle-flow candidates. A track is one particle seen twice; a calorimeter
cluster is a small, geometrically predictable set of particles. Once every
generated particle has its PF partner(s), two objects follow with no jet-level
pairing at all: the generated energy inside a reconstructed jet, weighted the
way PUPPI weighted its candidates (the GenJetSeed), and the reconstructed
energy that a generated jet's particles produced, clustered the way the
reconstruction would cluster it (the RecoJetSeed).

\section{The algorithm}
\label{s:algorithm}

\subsection{Input}
Per event: the PF candidates ($\pt$, $\eta$, $\phi$, $m$, pdgId, charge, $dz$
to the primary vertex, the key \texttt{vertexRef} of the reconstructed vertex
with its fit-quality flags \texttt{fromPVvertexRef} and
\texttt{pvAssocQuality}); the
primary-vertex $z$; the generated particles of every interaction with their
interaction index and vertex $z$. The reader (\texttt{nanoreader.h}) detects
the number of interaction slots from the branch list. Neutrinos are dropped;
particles beyond $|\eta| = $ \texttt{etaMaxPart} $= 5.5$ are dropped.

\subsection{Vertices}
Only the first four vertex positions are stored in NanoAOD, so the $z$ of every
reconstructed vertex is recovered from the median of $z_{\mathrm{PV}} + dz$
over the tracks that carry its key (25\,$\mu$m for the primary vertex, 49 for
the others; \texttt{puppi::VertexZ}). A vertex with fewer than
\texttt{minTrkPerVertex} $= 3$ tracks is unusable. A reconstructed vertex
\emph{owns} the generated interaction nearest to it if that is within
\texttt{dzVertexGen} $= 0.05$\,cm; an interaction is processed on the generated
side at the usable vertex nearest to it, if that is within
\texttt{dzRecoSeed} $= 0.2$\,cm. An interaction without such a vertex has no
$\Delta R$ partner, topology, mutual-dominance partner or RecoJetSeed, and its
pure jets are not in the efficiency denominators or the gen-side topology.

\subsection{PUPPI per vertex}
\texttt{puppi.h} recomputes the PUPPI weights $w_i(v)$ with any vertex $v$
playing the primary one: $\alpha_i = \log\sum_{j\ne i,\,\Delta R<0.4}
(\pt^j/\Delta R_{ij})^2$ over the charged candidates of $v$ inside the tracker
and over all candidates beyond it, $\chi^2_i = (\alpha_i - \mathrm{med})|\alpha_i
- \mathrm{med}|/\mathrm{rms}^2$, where med is the median of $\alpha$ over the
charged pileup candidates in the tracker and the 20\% quantile over all
candidates of each forward region, and rms is $0.7$ times the RMS of those
$\alpha$ about med taken on the low side only (candidates below 0.1\,GeV set
neither; \texttt{puppi::Config} \texttt{medQuantileFwd}, \texttt{rmsScale},
\texttt{leftRMS}, \texttt{rmsPtMin}),
$w_i = F_{\chi^2,1}(\chi^2_i)$, charged-from-$v$ candidates at 1 and charged
from elsewhere at 0, and a neutral floor $w\pt < 0.184 + 0.0131\,N_{\mathrm{PV}}$
($1.85 + 0.070\,N_{\mathrm{PV}}$ beyond $|\eta| = 2.5$). The free parameters were
read off the weights stored in NanoAOD against the primary vertex
(\texttt{puppi.h} header); the charged-to-vertex rule is \texttt{vertexRef}
with the fit flags, or $|z_{\mathrm{track}} - z_v| < 0.03$\,cm. Beyond
$|\eta| = 2.5$ the algorithm is vertex-blind by construction.

\subsection{The linker}
\label{s:linker}
\texttt{genlink.h} runs once per event, independently of the vertex hypothesis.

\paragraph{Geometry.} A PF track's direction is measured at the track, so its
target is the generated direction. A PF neutral's direction is built from the
reconstructed primary vertex, while the particle left interaction $j$ at
$z_j$; seen from $z_{\mathrm{PV}}$ the same crystal is at $\Delta\eta = (z_j -
z_{\mathrm{PV}})/(r\cosh\eta)$, 0.045 rms in the barrel. Every calorimeter
target is therefore the crossing point of the particle with the ECAL
($r = 1.29$\,m, $|\eta| < 1.479$, else $z = 3.17$\,m), HCAL (1.90\,m, 1.30, 3.90\,m)
or HF ($z = 11.15$\,m) surface, re-referenced to $z_{\mathrm{PV}}$
(\texttt{parallaxRef} $= 1$). A charged particle bends: $x = 0.2998\,B\,r/2\pt$,
$\phi_D = \phi - q\,\mathrm{bendSign}\,\arcsin x$, $z_D = z_j + r\sinh\eta\,\arcsin(x)/x$,
and at $x \ge 1$ it never reaches the surface (a barrel hadron below 0.735\,GeV
never sees the ECAL, below 1.08\,GeV never the HCAL); in HF the bend accrues
only to $r = z_{\mathrm{sol}}/|\sinh\eta|$ with $z_{\mathrm{sol}} = 3.4$\,m.
Both are checked on the data by \texttt{drawGenSeed.C}: the slope of
$\eta_{\mathrm{PF}} - \eta_{\mathrm{gen}}$ against $(z_j - z_{\mathrm{PV}})/(r\cosh\eta)$
for single-source photon links must be $+1$, and the slope of $q\,\Delta\phi$
against $1/\pt$ for untracked-hadron links must be negative, of order
$-0.7$ to $-1$\,rad\,GeV.

\paragraph{Pass 1, prompt tracks, one to one.} Generated charged particles
with $c\tau > 1$\,m ($\pi^\pm$, $K^\pm$, p, e, $\mu$) and PF tracks (pdgId 211,
11, 13) inside $|\eta| \le 3$: same charge sign (\texttt{useCharge}),
$\Delta R < 0.03$ (0.05 beyond $|\eta| = 1.5$), $|\ln(\pt^{\mathrm{PF}}/\pt^{\mathrm{gen}})| < 0.7$,
$|z_{\mathrm{track}} - z_j| < \max(0.10\,\mathrm{cm},\, 3\sigma_z)$ with
$\sigma_z = \sqrt{0.003^2 + (0.02\cosh^{1.5}\eta/\pt)^2}$\,cm (\texttt{useDz};
0.30\,cm floor for $2.5 < |\eta| < 3$). Pairs are scored by
$\chi^2 = (\Delta R/0.008)^2 + (\Delta z/\sigma_z)^2 + (\ln\mathrm{ratio}/0.15)^2$,
sorted, and accepted greedily while both ends are free. A taken track carries
nothing else.

\paragraph{Pass 2, calorimeter and HF, shared.} Every particle without a pass-1
link is partitioned over the candidates inside a class-dependent cone at its
target, kernel $a_i = C\exp(-\Delta R^2/2\sigma^2)$, $f_{gi} = a_i/\sum a$, so
that a linked particle is fully linked ($\sum_i f_{gi} = 1$). The table
($\sigma$ / cut / compatibility $C$; $\sigma$ and cut scaled by
\texttt{kernelScale} and by 1.4 for $1.5 < |\eta| < 3$, the cut then clamped at
0.4, the cell of the linker's search grid, so that the $V^0$ cone at
$1.5<|\eta|<3$ ($0.35\times1.4$) and the HF cone at $2.9<|\eta|<3$
($0.30\times1.4$) are at the clamp already for \texttt{kernelScale} $=1$):
photon $\to$ PF photon
at the ECAL 0.025 / 0.06 / 1.0, $\to$ neutral hadron at the HCAL 0.06 / 0.15 / 0.3,
$\to$ unclaimed track (conversion) 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.3; neutral hadron $\to$ PF
neutral hadron 0.08 / 0.20 / 1.0, $\to$ photon 0.04 / 0.10 / 0.3; untracked
charged hadron $\to$ photon at the bent ECAL target 0.04 / 0.10 / 0.5, $\to$
neutral hadron at the bent HCAL target 0.08 / 0.20 / 1.0, $\to$ unclaimed
same-sign track within 0.05 and 0.3\,cm (decay in flight) 0.5; $V^0$ ($K_S$,
$\Lambda$, hyperons) $\to$ unclaimed tracks of either sign, photons, neutral
hadrons within $\min(0.35,\, 0.05 + 0.45/\pt)$ at 1.0 / 0.7 / 1.0; beyond
$|\eta| = 2.9$ everything $\to$ HF candidates beyond 2.85 within 0.30, natural
(hadron $\to$ HF hadron, $\gamma$/e $\to$ HF em) 1.0, crossed 0.5. The
\emph{tier rule}: if a candidate with $C = 1$ is inside the cone, the $C < 1$
candidates are dropped. There is no rescue pass: a PF candidate with no
generated partner is an orphan and is counted (1--2\% of the jet $\pt$ on this
sample), not attached to the nearest particle at full $\pt$, which would move
detector response and pileup into the seed by construction.

\paragraph{Output.} For every PF candidate $i$ the routed generated
four-vector $G_i = \sum_g f_{gi}\, p_g$ (generated $\pt$, generated mass), the
link lists both ways, and one record per link for the diagnostics.

\subsection{GenJetSeed}
\label{s:genjetseed}
For each usable vertex $v$ the PF candidates with $w_i(v) > 0$ are clustered
(anti-$k_{\mathrm T}$, $R = $ \texttt{R} $= 0.4$, from
\texttt{ptMinCluster} $= 1$\,GeV) together with ghosts ($\pt \times 10^{-9}$)
for the \emph{unlinked} particles of the interaction $v$ owns. The seed of jet
$k$ is
\[
  \vec S_k = \sum_{i \in k} w_i(v)\, G_i \;+\; \sum_{\text{unlinked own ghosts } g \in k} p_g ,
  \qquad \ptlink = |\vec S_{k,\mathrm T}| ,
\]
i.e.\ a linked particle is credited to the jet its PF partner ended up in,
with the partner's PUPPI weight, at generated scale; an unlinked own particle
is credited to the jet its direction falls in, at weight 1; an unlinked far
particle to nothing. \texttt{linkz} is the first sum alone. The seed is
decomposed into own / far (by interaction) / unlinked parts and the
reconstructed jet into attributed / orphan parts. Because $\pt^{\mathrm{PF}}$
never enters, $R = \ptraw/\ptlink$ stays a detector response ---
calorimeter non-linearity, thresholds, tracking inefficiency, the neutral
floor --- and a correction derived from it transfers to data.

\subsection{RecoJetSeed}
\label{s:recojetseed}
For each pure generated jet $J$ of interaction $j$ (anti-$k_{\mathrm T}$
$R$, clustered per interaction from \texttt{ptMinGen} $= 1$\,GeV) with a
usable vertex within \texttt{dzRecoSeed} $= 0.2$\,cm, the PF candidates linked
to its particles are collected with the share
\[
  s_{iJ} = \frac{\sum_{g \in J} f_{gi}\, \pt^g}{S_i} \in [0,1] ,
  \qquad S_i = \sum_g f_{gi}\, \pt^g ,
\]
the fraction of candidate $i$'s attributed (scalar) generated $\pt$ that came
from $J$. The shares of one candidate sum to at most 1 over the pure jets, so
a candidate shared with another jet or with pileup is never credited twice
(dividing by the magnitude $G_{i,\mathrm T}$ of the vector sum instead, which is
smaller for partners in different directions, would do that). Each candidate
enters as $s_{iJ}\, w_i(v)\, p_i^{\mathrm{PF}}$ with the PUPPI weight at that
vertex, the set is clustered with anti-$k_{\mathrm T}$ $R$ (no threshold), and the hardest
jet is the RecoJetSeed: its four-vector, the number of candidates,
$f_{\mathrm{lead}}$ (its $\pt$ over the scalar sum of the weighted candidates),
$f_{\mathrm{linked}}$ (the linked fraction of $J$'s generated $\pt$) and
$f_{\mathrm{PUPPI}}$ ($\sum s w \pt / \sum s \pt$, what PUPPI kept of $J$'s
candidates). The same candidates clustered at weight $s_{iJ}$ alone give
$\pt^{\mathrm{rs0}}$, the jet as the detector saw it before PUPPI decided
anything; the gap between $\pt^{\mathrm{rs0}}$ and $\ptrs$ is what PUPPI took
away.

$\ptrs/\ptpure$ is what the detector made of \emph{this} generated jet: no
$\Delta R$, no share threshold, no dedup, no other interaction's particles. It
is not efficiency-free, though: where PUPPI keeps none of $J$'s candidates
(most forward jets of a few GeV) $\ptrs = 0$, the jet is not in the response,
and the response is to be read together with the fraction that has a
RecoJetSeed (\texttt{eff/hgmat\_rs}); $\pt^{\mathrm{rs0}}/\ptpure$ is filled
wherever anything of $J$ was linked. Because the RecoJetSeed is built from a
subset of the jet's own candidates, its response sits below 1 with a flat low
side: the tail is on the left, and the v4 core fit, whose window assumes a
right tail, is often undefined for it (section~\ref{s:results}). The
reconstructed jet then factorises as
\[
  \frac{\ptraw}{\ptpure} = \frac{\ptrs}{\ptpure} \times \frac{\ptraw}{\ptrs} ,
\]
with $\ptraw$ that of the kept copy of $J$'s mutual-dominance partner
(section~\ref{s:topology}): the second factor is what pileup and the clustering
of the whole event add. No correction is applied to $\ptrs$: the reco-jet
table maps $\ptraw$ to the link scale, which a RecoJetSeed is not on.

\subsection{Topology and the bridge}
\label{s:topology}
Reconstructed side, per vertex and seed $k$: the pure jets giving more than
\texttt{shareFrac} $= 1/3$ of $\ptlink$ are its contributors, $n_{\mathrm{in}}$;
$p^*$ is the largest; $n_{\mathrm{out}}$ counts the jets receiving more than
\texttt{shareFrac} of $p^*$. Classes: none ($n_{\mathrm{in}} = 0$),
$1\leftrightarrow1$ (1, 1), split (1, $\ne 1$), merge ($\ge2$, 1), tangle
($\ge2$, $\ge2$); they partition the reconstructed jets. Generated side, per
pure jet $J$ at its vertex: $n_r$ jets receive more than \texttt{shareFrac} of
$\ptpure$ and $k_{\mathrm{main}}$ is the one receiving most; lost ($n_r = 0$),
$1\leftrightarrow1$ ($n_r = 1$, $k_{\mathrm{main}}$ has at most one
contributor), split ($\ge2$, at most one), merge (1, $\ge2$), tangle ($\ge2$,
$\ge2$); they partition the pure jets whose interaction has a vertex (a jet
without one keeps \texttt{gj\_topo} $=$ lost in the tuple but is not in
\texttt{topo/}). A seed $k$ and a pure jet $J$ are \emph{mutually dominant}
when each gives the other more than \texttt{shareFrac} and each is the
other's largest: $J$ gave $k$ more than \texttt{shareFrac} of $\ptlink$ and is
its $p^*$, and $k$ received more than \texttt{shareFrac} of $\ptpure$ and is
$J$'s $k_{\mathrm{main}}$. It is one relation, \texttt{rj\_bpair} on the
reconstructed side and the dominance partner on the generated side, so the
dominance efficiency counts exactly the pure jets that the fake rates and the
bridge see paired. The bridge from the seed level to the pure level is written
as a matrix over the mutual pairs with the unpaired seeds (pileup-made) and
unpaired pure jets (no vertex, lost) as additive vectors. Jets reconstructed
at several vertex hypotheses ($\Delta R < $ \texttt{dupDR} $= 0.15$, $|\Delta\pt| <$
\texttt{dupPtFrac} $= 0.10$) are kept once, the copy with the largest own
fraction.

\subsection{The clustering backend}
\label{s:backend}
\texttt{tiledjet.h} is an independent implementation of the sequential
recombination algorithms (anti-$k_{\mathrm T}$, $k_{\mathrm T}$,
Cambridge--Aachen through the exponent $p$) with the tiled strategy of
Cacciari and Salam: tiles of size $\ge R$ in rapidity and azimuth, nearest
neighbours searched in the $3\times3$ block around a particle, a min-heap over
the candidate distances, and after each merge a full rescan only for the
particles whose nearest neighbour was one of the merged pair. It contains no
FastJet code. \texttt{bench\_tiledjet.C} checks it against a plain $N^2$
reference and against FastJet for exactness and speed; the verdict is compiled
into \texttt{tiledjet::kAtLeastAsFastAsFastJet} and the driver uses FastJet
only when it is found and the verdict is false. \input{benchmark}

\section{The histogram file}
\label{s:histos}
Directory \texttt{hist} holds the counters and the knobs, and next to them,
as text, what is not a number: the version of \texttt{puppi.h} the weights
were made with (\texttt{puppiVersion}) and the correction table, or
\texttt{none} in pass 1 (\texttt{jecFile}); \texttt{spec} the spectra
of the four collections per $|y|$ bin and half; \texttt{eff} the pairing
efficiencies ($\Delta R<0.2$, mutual dominance, link topology, RecoJetSeed
present); \texttt{fake} the reconstructed jets with no pure feeder and without
a mutual partner; \texttt{resp} the response distributions as
$(\pt^{\mathrm{def}}, R)$ histograms for $\ptraw/\ptlink$
(\texttt{hresp\_link}), $\ptraw/\pt^{\mathrm{linkz}}$ (\texttt{hresp\_linkz}),
$\ptraw/\ptpure$ ($\Delta R$ pairs, \texttt{hresp\_dr}), $\ptrs/\ptpure$
(\texttt{hresp\_rs}, for $\ptrs>0$), $\pt^{\mathrm{rs0}}/\ptpure$
(\texttt{hresp\_rs0}, the RecoJetSeed without PUPPI weights, for
$\pt^{\mathrm{rs0}}>0$) and $\ptraw/\ptrs$ against $\ptpure$
(\texttt{hresp\_reco\_rs}, the kept copy of the mutual-dominance partner over
the RecoJetSeed: the pileup and clustering factor of
$\mathrm{reco}/\mathrm{pure} = \mathrm{rs}/\mathrm{pure}\times\mathrm{reco}/\mathrm{rs}$),
the link response by reconstructed topology and by $N_{\mathrm{PU}}$ slice,
with the seed-fraction profiles (files written before these two were added have
\texttt{hresp\_rs\_corr}, the RecoJetSeed times the reco-jet JEC, which is
not a closure and is no longer made); \texttt{topo} the topology counts both sides;
\texttt{bridge} the seed-to-pure matrix and its vectors; \texttt{map} the
$\eta$--$\phi$ maps (jet densities, response profiles, orphan and linked
fractions, far fraction); \texttt{link} the linker diagnostics. The
$\pt$ axis is the standard CMS inclusive-jet binning extended down to 2\,GeV
(edges 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 28, \ldots, 790); $|\eta|$ and
$|y|$ bins are 0.5 wide to 5.0; the response axis is 0--5 in 500 bins.
Halves a and b are the even and odd entries, for closure tests.
