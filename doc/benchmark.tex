Table~\ref{tab:bench} is the result of
\texttt{root -l -b -q 'bench\_tiledjet.C(200,"NANOAODSIM\_1.root")'} on
2026-09-23: 200 events of the sample, anti-$k_{\mathrm T}$ $R=0.4$, E-scheme,
jets above 1\,GeV compared, on an Apple M2 (4 performance + 4 efficiency
cores, macOS 13.5, Apple clang 15, ACLiC \texttt{-O3}) against FastJet 3.5.1
with its \texttt{Best} strategy. The four inputs span the multiplicities the
analyzer meets: the PUPPI-weighted candidates of one vertex hypothesis, all PF
candidates unweighted, the same jittered by $10^{-7}$, and all candidates plus
the gen ghosts of every interaction. tiledjet is 13--29\% faster on every
input, which is why it is the default. It is identical to the $N^2$ reference
on every jet of every input (four-vectors bitwise, constituent lists in order),
and identical to FastJet on every jet of the PUPPI, jittered and ghost inputs;
on the unweighted PF input 505 jets in 27 of 200 events differ from FastJet
(and, identically, the $N^2$ reference does) because HF towers and ECAL
crystals at exactly equal float $\eta$ or $\phi$ give exactly tied distances,
and which tied pair merges first is a convention. A $10^{-7}$ jitter removes
every difference. A sanitizer fuzz against the $N^2$ reference (6600 random
events, $N \le 3000$, $0.2 \le R \le 6.2$, $p = -1, 0, 1$, duplicates, zero
$\pt$, $10^{-9}$ ghosts, particles within a few ulps of tile boundaries) finds
no difference.
